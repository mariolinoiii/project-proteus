Mapping of Suspected Oil Slicks using SAR & GeoAI


ProteusSegNet is an integrated workspace for screening SAR imagery for suspected oil slicks. It combines dataset preparation, model training and scene analysis in one workflow, built around a custom convolutional segmentation network.


Developed around the GEM No.2 case study for the National Space Academy 2026 Earth Observation Application Development (Level 2) course, in anticipation of the 2026 UN Water Conference in Abu Dhabi. Thematic alignment includes SDGs 14, 6, 13 and 9. No government endorsement or operational certification is implied.

From SAR to analyst review
Screening workflow — not spill confirmation.
  1. Upload SAR: calibrated, single-band backscatter in dB with a CRS.
  2. Align WorldCover: identify reference water; exclude unknown coverage.
  3. Prepare labels: generate physics-based suspected-oil pseudo-labels.
  4. Train ProteusSegNet: fit the segmentation network to those labels.
  5. Analyse: apply the physics criteria and model-score threshold.
  6. Review and export: inspect candidates, figures, PDF and GIS products.

Scope and limits
  • Helps locate candidate features and prioritise them for visual review.
  • Does not confirm spills, attribute vessels, establish offences or quantify environmental harm.
  • Look-alikes (low wind, biogenic films, rain) can resemble oil, and model scores are not probabilities.
